# Privacy Policy

**Games Tracker for LinkedIn**

Last updated: July 27, 2026

## Data Collection

This extension collects only the game results you achieve on LinkedIn games (Pinpoint, Queens,
Crossclimb, Tango, Wend, Patches, Zip, Mini-Sudoku). This includes scores, completion times, and
dates of play.

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using your browser's IndexedDB. No data is transmitted to
any external server, third party, or the extension developer.

## Data Sharing

This extension does not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties.

## Permissions

The extension runs content scripts on linkedin.com pages solely to read completed game results and
the names of friends shown on leaderboards. It does not track browsing activity or access any other
personal information.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the extension or clearing its site data
through your browser's extension settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue on the
